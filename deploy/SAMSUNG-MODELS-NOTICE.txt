VisionGate uses public PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 mobile detection and Latin recognition
models from PaddlePaddle, licensed under Apache-2.0. The license is distributed
alongside this notice. These are upstream public weights, not weights trained
on Samsung examples. No corporate photographs or annotations are included.

Sources:
https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv5_mobile_det_onnx
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/latin_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec_onnx

Exact revisions, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in config/samsung-models.json.
Inference uses installed local weights and has no network model fallback.
